Privacy, in plain words

Short version: Dame needs no account. The only thing about you we ever keep is a nickname you choose, and, if you pick one, a campus, school or region for the leaderboards. Please don’t use your full name.

Kept only on your phone

  • Your settings, nickname, line-ups, streaks and unlocked characters. They sit in your browser’s storage and never leave your phone on their own. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Sent to our server (only when you use online features)

  • Playing a friend live: the room code, the rules, the moves and the result. Your nickname travels between the two phones while you play but isn’t saved. Rooms close after 2 hours and are deleted the next day.
  • Play over days: the moves, both nicknames and whose turn it is. A game is deleted 14 days after its last move. If you turn on “your turn” notifications, we keep the notification address your browser gives us for that game only, and delete it when the game ends.
  • Chat: messages go straight from phone to phone and are not stored. If you tap report, that one message, the sender’s nickname and the room code are saved so we can review it. Reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Leaderboards and leagues: your nickname, your group (if you chose one), a random player number made on your phone so leagues can tell two “Kofi”s apart, and your points for the week. Scores are deleted after 12 weeks.
  • Anonymous counts: we add one to a daily total when a game starts or ends, a puzzle is played or shared, or an invite link is opened. No name, no device ID, nothing that points to you. You can switch this off in Settings, and we skip it if your browser sends “Do Not Track”.

What we never collect

No real names, phone numbers, emails, contacts, location, photos or advertising IDs. No cookies. No ads are running — the “Ad space” boxes are empty placeholders.

Who else is involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the game files and Supabase runs the database. Like any web host, they can see your internet address when your phone connects.
  • Google Fonts supplies the handwriting fonts the first time you load the game.
  • Sharing goes through your phone’s own share sheet or WhatsApp; we don’t see what you send.

Reporting abuse

  • In a chat, tap report next to the message. That also mutes the player for you.
  • You can tap Mute at any time to hide someone’s messages.
  • For anything else — a rude nickname on the leaderboard, a safety worry, or a request to remove something — open an issue on GitHub with the nickname and roughly when it happened. Don’t post your own personal details there.

Children

Dame is for everyone. Because there are no accounts and chat isn’t stored, the game can’t know anyone’s age; please play online with people you know, and never share personal details in chat.
