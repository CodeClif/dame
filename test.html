Dame is Ghanaian draughts in a hand-drawn notebook: Forest Friends against the Market Crew. The characters come alive when they move. The crocodile bites, the spider slings webs, the elephant stomps. Thanks for playing a few games and telling us how it went!

The test link unlocks every character for your visit. You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, number or email.

Try these

  • Play the computer as Forest Friends. Capture with the crocodile, spider, parrot and tortoise to see their secret skills.
  • Pass and play with someone next to you, on one phone.
  • Do the daily puzzle.
  • Open How to play. Is anything confusing?
  • In Settings, switch Character animations off and on, and try night mode.
  • Optional: add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there, even with no internet.
