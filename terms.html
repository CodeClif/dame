Terms, in plain words

  • Free to play. There are no entry fees, no stakes and no cash prizes — not in any mode, league or leaderboard. Please don’t play Dame for money.
  • Cosmetic only. Characters and packs only change how your pieces look. Every piece follows the same rules. The shop is a preview: nothing can be bought yet.
  • Be kind. No insults, threats, hate, or sharing anyone’s personal details in chat or in nicknames. We can remove nicknames, scores or games that break this.
  • Play fair. Leaderboards and leagues are just for fun. Scores that look faked may be removed, and tables may be reset.
  • Online games end. Live rooms close after 2 hours. Games played over days end after 14 days without a move: the player whose turn it was forfeits.
  • As it is. Dame is a small hobby game, provided as it is. We try to keep it running but can’t promise it will always be online or bug-free.
  • The art. The characters and drawings are original artwork made for Dame.

Questions or problems? Open an issue on GitHub. Read how your data is handled in the privacy page.
